Case Study 07 / Freelance · Web app

Anubis Law — state law exam prep.

Exam prep for the state law portion of the funeral service licensing exam. Designed for Anubis Publications. North Carolina first.

Look around the prototype →
roleProduct design — solo
clientAnubis Publications
statusIn development
01

Video checkpoints

Lessons are split into short segments. After each one, the video stops on a question. Answer it to keep watching.

02

Projected score

One number against the 75% pass mark: the last two practice exams averaged with the last three sessions. The weakest topic is named, and every card opens the next study action.

03

Practice exams

Exam conditions: flag questions to revisit, no feedback until you submit. Exams draw from a separate test bank, so dashboard stats aren't inflated by questions you've already seen.

04

Drills

Five-question quizzes that explain every answer. Flashcards and matching for citations and deadlines.

05

On phones

A bottom tab bar replaces the top nav.

06

Marketing site

Features, pricing, FAQ, and sign-up. One plan: $99 for six months of access.
