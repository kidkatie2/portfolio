01 — Problem

Two audiences, opposite goals.

Instructors assess students preparing for the National Board Exam; students drill, review, and compete. Shared: the question bank. Instructors think in cohorts, weighting, analytics; students in streaks, scores, due dates.

Earlier versions toggled one view. The redesign: two experiences on shared primitives.

06 — Decisions

Three decisions.

01

Two homepages, not one toggle.

Split routes: /student and /professor. An earlier single dashboard with a role toggle buried the primary content for both users.

02

Class Detail as five tabs, not five pages.

Roster, Grade Book, Assignments, Analytics, Questions share class context. Tabs keep one class header; analytics is one click from the gradebook.

03

A dev toolbar in the prototype itself.

A bottom-edge drawer with screen jump, UI-state toggle (default / empty / error), feature flags, and mobile-view sim. Engineering verifies states without designer involvement.

07 — Detail

The dev toolbar.

Every screen has empty, error, feature-flagged future, and 375px mobile states. The toolbar switches each with one click.
