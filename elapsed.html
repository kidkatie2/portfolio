Case Study 06 / Personal · iOS app

Elapsed — progress widgets.

Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets that show how much of the day, week, year, or life has passed — or the days left to a deadline. Designed and built solo under Tesoro Industries LLC.

TL;DR

Trackers as bars, rings, or dot grids. Free: two trackers, bar style. Pro: $3.99 once. Light and dark both free. No account, no backend, no analytics.

roleSolo — design & build
stackSwift · SwiftUI · WidgetKit
paymentsStoreKit 2
platformiPhone · Home + Lock Screen
priceFree / $3.99 Pro
year2026
01

The widgets

One configurable widget, five sizes. Pick a tracker and a style from the widget's own settings.

02

Trackers and styles

Templates

This Year, This Month, This Week, Today, Life, Countdown, Custom. Name it, pick a template, pick a style and a color.

Three styles

Bar, ring, dot grid. The preview at the top updates as you edit.

03

Pricing

Free: any two trackers, bar style. Pro, $3.99 once: unlimited trackers, ring and dot-grid styles, Lock Screen widgets. No subscription.

Light and dark are always free — you pay for styles, never for a mode. Widgets show Pro styles with a PRO badge instead of breaking, since a widget can't show a paywall.

04

Privacy

  • AccountNone
  • AnalyticsNone
  • BackendNone
  • StorageOn-device; shared only with the app's widgets
  • Privacy labelData Not Collected
